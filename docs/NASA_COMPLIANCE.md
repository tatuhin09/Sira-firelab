# NASA Space Apps Challenge Alignment

Challenge: **Flame in Freefall: SI-Powered Fire Safety Insights from Microgravity Combustion Data**

Official challenge: https://www.spaceappschallenge.org/2026/challenges/flame-in-freefall/

## Challenge objective and implementation

SIRA FireLab is an interactive research workspace for discovering, inspecting, summarizing, ranking, and interpreting microgravity combustion evidence. It provides separate NASA FLEX and BASS-II explorers, deterministic descriptive analyses, bounded rankings, source-linked synthesis, research planning, and claim-level verification.

| Challenge objective | FireLab implementation |
|---|---|
| Interactive dashboard | Ask SIRA, Overview, Data Explorer, BASS-II Explorer, Research Lab, Methods & Sources |
| Find combustion research | Separate NASA FLEX and BASS-II explorers |
| Summarize and rank | Registered descriptive summaries and bounded within-metric rankings |
| Interpret evidence | Research planning, Claim Ledger, source-linked synthesis, scientific verifier |
| Preserve traceability | Release identifiers, hashes, record keys, lineage checks, caveats |

## NASA challenge Resources mapping

The official Flame in Freefall Challenge Resources list names this NASA source:

**PSI Data Repository: Combustion Science**  
https://psi.nasa.gov/physci/repo/search?q=combustion

FireLab uses two investigation datasets hosted by that NASA Physical Sciences Informatics repository:

1. **PSI-69 — Flame Extinguishment Experiment (FLEX), Version 5**; DOI 10.60555/mbq8-0451; 274 bundled records. Official investigation page: https://psi.nasa.gov/physci/repo/data/investigations/PSI-69
2. **PSI-25 — Burning and Suppression of Solids II (BASS-II), Study Version 4**; DOI 10.60555/4qc4-de67; 129 bundled records. Official investigation page: https://psi.nasa.gov/physci/repo/data/investigations/PSI-25

**Scope precision:** the challenge Resources entry names the PSI repository at repository level. It does not separately enumerate PSI-69 or PSI-25. Cite the challenge-listed repository entry and cite each exact investigation page and DOI separately. Do not claim that the challenge list individually names either investigation.

## Scientific scope

FireLab is a research evidence workspace. It does not claim operational flammability prediction, spacecraft fire-control recommendations, certified fire-safety limits, or universal material safety rankings. Descriptive cohort differences are not causal effects.
